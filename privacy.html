Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what LEVEL UP LOGIC LTD ("we", "us") does with information when you visit leveluplogic.games or play one of our games: Bloodline Warriors, Expendable Heroes, Iron Pit Football and Doubletopia 2048. We are the data controller. Contact: info@leveluplogic.games.

The short version

  • Our games have no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name or email address.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored on your own device. We cannot see them.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Some games use services from Apple, Google and other companies, listed below. Those companies handle the data described under their own policies.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms or accounts. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. If you email us, we keep your message so that we can reply.

Our games

Stored on your device

Each game saves things like settings, progress, unlocks, best scores and daily challenge history in your device's local storage. This stays on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Game Center and Google Play Games

Where a game offers leaderboards or achievements and you choose to connect, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center or Google Play Games. Connecting is optional. See the Apple and Google privacy policies.

Advertising

Free versions of some games may show adverts supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location, and information about the device, app and adverts, to show and measure adverts and, if you agree, to personalise them. We do not receive this data.

  • In the UK and EEA, the game asks for your choice using Google's consent form before the first advert. Refusing means non-personalised adverts.
  • On iOS, the game asks permission before any tracking identifier is used.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

More: how Google uses information in advertising.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as removing adverts, are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Some games use RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase exists; it receives an anonymous purchase identifier, not your name, email or card. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Crash reports

Some games may send a crash report to Sentry when the game fails, so that the bug can be fixed. A report holds the error, the app version, the device model and operating system version, and a random installation identifier. It holds no name, email address or advertising identifier. See the Sentry privacy policy.

Doubletopia 2048

Doubletopia 2048 has no accounts and keeps your scores, themes and progress on your device. It shows Google AdMob full-screen adverts between games, after the iOS tracking prompt and, in the UK and EEA, Google's consent form. Doubletopia Plus, a one-off purchase handled by Apple or Google, removes adverts; RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier to confirm it. If the game crashes it may send an anonymous crash report to Sentry. Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, only if you connect.

Expendable Heroes

Expendable Heroes has no accounts. Progress, settings and scores are saved on your device. Free play shows Google AdMob full-screen adverts between levels, after Google's consent form in the UK and EEA and the iOS tracking prompt. A one-off purchase handled by Apple or Google removes adverts. Sharing a result card or clip goes through your device's share sheet only when you choose.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and several contain cartoon violence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Legal basis

Advertising data is processed by Google on the basis of your consent. Anything we process ourselves, such as replying to your email, is for our legitimate interest in running the games and the company.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Because the games keep your data on your device and we hold no accounts, there is usually nothing for us to delete: uninstalling the game removes it. For data held by Apple, Google, RevenueCat or Sentry, use their own controls. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this page when a game gains a feature that changes what is described here, and change the date at the top.

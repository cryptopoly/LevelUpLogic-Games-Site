Support

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Need help with one of our games (Bloodline Warriors, Expendable Heroes, Iron Pit Football or Doubletopia 2048)? Email info@leveluplogic.games and we will reply as soon as we can.

What to include

  • The name of the game and its version (usually in the game's settings).
  • Your device and its operating system version, for example "iPhone 15, iOS 19".
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

I lost my progress

Our games keep your progress, scores and settings on your device, and we hold no accounts. Deleting a game, or moving to a new device without a device backup, removes them, and we cannot restore them for you. Where a game offers Game Center or Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores and achievements are kept there.

I bought something and it is missing

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. In a game with a purchase, open its purchase screen and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought with. If that does not work, email us with the name of the game and your order receipt from Apple or Google.

Refunds

Apple and Google process refunds, not us. Use Apple's Report a Problem page or Google Play's refund help.

Adverts and privacy choices

Where a game shows adverts, you can change your consent choice from the game's settings, and reset your advertising ID in your device settings. See our privacy policy.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Email us. We read everything, even if we cannot answer every message.
